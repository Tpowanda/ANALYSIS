    Glossary

    Discount depth
    Average % off the ticket price across units sold.
    Normal range
    Mean ± 2 (or 3) standard deviations of the prior 8, 13 or 26 weeks.
    z-score
    How many standard deviations a week sits from normal. Beyond ±2 is unusual.
    Break-even lift
    Extra units needed to earn the same gross profit: depth ÷ (margin − depth).
    Coverage
    Actual or predicted lift ÷ break-even lift. Below 1.0× loses money.
    Price elasticity
    % change in units for a 1% change in price paid. −2 means a 10% cut sells about 23% more.
    Pull-forward
    A deep promotion that borrows sales from the weeks after it.
    Price index
    Gap average price ÷ peer average × 100. 100 = priced like peers.
    Price realized
    Price actually paid ÷ ticket price.

    Method and data notes

    • Source: Discount_Budget_KPI_Workbook.xlsx (28 sheets, ~22,600 formulas recalculated with zero errors) and its SQL Server query set.
    • Simulated: 52 weeks of sales, cost and customer data for a Gap-brand online channel scaled to public FY2025 figures.
    • Real: competitor list and shown prices (611 listings, 3 Nov 2026, one-day snapshot) and public KPI benchmarks cited in the workbook.
    • Live in this page: rolling means, standard deviations, z-scores, run rules, break-even, gross-profit change, approval tiers and the simulator. Elasticity fits, depth bands, scenarios and price summaries are read from the workbook.
    • Validation: this page's 13-week figures reproduce the workbook's Std Dev sheet (e.g. latest discount depth z = +4.09).
    • Limits: elasticity ignores returns and brand effects; overlapping pull-forward windows make z a rough guide. Confirm with a hold-out test before acting.
